The Self-Enclosing Line

A 2.5-D reconstruction of the elevated deck's visual & solar field — and a test of whether the park built the walls that now enclose it.
Method. Ray-cast isovist over 176 building prisms (real heights + construction year); Oke uniform-sky SVF; solar-geometry shadow accretion at lat 40.74°. The self-enclosure band isolates sky & sun removed by the 6 towers built after the park's 2009 opening — a conservative floor. Deck values are a geometric estimate; the CV street audit is an independent layer (they proved uncorrelated, r≈0 — different vantage, different occluders), not a calibration. Flat-topped prisms, clear sky, no street trees.
